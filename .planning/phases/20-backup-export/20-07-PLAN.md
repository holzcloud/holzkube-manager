Die Formen der API (Pläne 05 und 06, im Vertrag in docs/api-contract.md):
- `GET` und `PUT /api/v1/backup-export` → `{"configured", "target": null | {"kind","endpoint","bucket","region","prefix","addressing","ca_pem","since","rev"}, "secret_set", "credentials_since", "notice"}`; `PUT` nimmt `{"kind":"s3","endpoint","bucket","region","prefix","addressing","ca_pem","access_key_id","secret_access_key"}`, die zwei letzten beide oder keines, nie in einer Antwort; `DELETE` gibt 204

- `GET /api/v1/clusters/{id}/backups` bekommt `"export": {"configured", "health": {enabled, overdue, last_success_at, newest_exported_at, age_seconds, pending, failed, lost, last_result, last_reason, verified}, "snapshots": [{id, state, at, verified, reason, attempts, next_try_at}], "notice"}`; `state` ist `exported`, `exporting`, `pending`, `failed` oder `not-configured`; `verified` ist `response`, `head`, `size`, `none` oder leer
