<objective>
Close the badge and recovery half of G-02-1, give G-02-9 the mitigation its ratified decision asks
for, and write down — accurately — what this round did and did not move.

Purpose: a re-POST of an identical customisation already runs the whole `Author` sequence, including
a fresh probe that usually succeeds because the first attempt warmed the Factory, and then throws the
verdict away. G-02-9 measured it: `HTTP 409 in 4.186898875s`, the probe ran and succeeded at that
latency, the record stayed `usable=false` with `probed_at` zero. The comment at that branch refuses to
overwrite the label, the cluster and the version, and that reasoning is sound — a `POST` the contract
marks `Destructive: false` must not do a destructive route's damage. It does not extend to the probe
verdict, which is not the operator's text and is not a thing the second POST could disagree with.

That is the whole of the change: on conflict, write `Usable`, `ProbedAt` and `ProbeReason`, and
nothing else. No route, no probe state, no field, no changed status code — the four things that
distinguish the ratified Option 2 from the Option 1 the user did not take.

The rest of this plan is bookkeeping in the strict sense: making the statements in the tree match what
the tree now does. Three places say a second POST is refused rather than merged, and after this
change that is true of the label, the cluster and the version and no longer true of the verdict. The
badge's no-verdict copy tells the operator nothing about a recovery that now exists. And the ledger
still carries entries 20 and 48, one of which warns in capitals that plan 02-19 changed what an
unresolved installer *returns* and not the cold serial walk that produces it. This round is the one
that finally touched that walk, and the ledger has to say precisely what moved.

One item on the decision's unconditional list is **already discharged and is not re-planned here**:
the badge no longer claims the probe "did not run" when it ran and timed out. Plan 02-12 closed that
in the same commit as G-02-3, the copy now reads *Not verified — the build probe has no verdict* with
a muted line stating the disjunction, and 02-UAT.md test 1's `badge_half` records that the old
sentence has zero occurrences in the shipped bundle and that no Go string produces it. What this plan
adds to that branch is the recovery it could not name while the decision was open, and the correction
of the comment there that still says the decision is open.

Output: a conflict that keeps the verdict it computed, three narrowed statements, copy that names the
recovery without promising a verdict, and a ledger whose entries this round leaves accurate — G-02-9
among them, recorded as open.
