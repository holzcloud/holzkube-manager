<objective>
Close G-02-7, and close the state half of G-02-8: stop the asset panel's architecture from
redefining what the next schematic is built and probed against, and give the schematic detail dialog
an error branch.

**Scope note, load-bearing.** G-02-8 has two `missing` bullets and this plan closes the first. The
second — persisting the probed architecture on the record and showing it beside the Usability
verdict — is plan 02-13's, which runs in gap-closure wave 4 after 02-12 has rewritten the verdict
sentence that an architecture would qualify, and which carries `gap_ids: [G-02-8]`. So the gap is
closed by the pair, and the frontmatter here claims `G-02-7` alone — not because the remainder is
deferred, but because a gap must be claimed by the plan that finishes it, and this one does not.

Purpose: one `useRememberedArch()` state at `images.tsx:80` is bound both to the creation form's
Architecture select and, through `SchematicDetail`, to the asset panel — and it is persisted to
localStorage. Switching the panel to arm64 to read arm64 URLs silently changed the architecture the
next schematic was probed against, permanently. Separately, `SchematicDetail` renders exactly two
branches, `isSuccess` and `isPending`, so a deleted or unreachable record opens a modal whose entire
text content is the word "Close" — measured, `visibleTextLength: 5`.

Output: an asset panel that owns its own architecture, a detail dialog that says why it cannot show
a schematic, and a saved list that drops a row the server no longer has.
</objective>
