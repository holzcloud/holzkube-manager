<objective>

Proving the path end to end on a single series first catches a wrong seam (a second ticker, a sample that shares the page's rate memo, a route in the wrong place) after one commit, not after eight. Every later plan widens this path; none builds a new one.
Output: `HostSubject`, `SamplerDeps.Host`, `Collector.Sample` + `HistoryValues` (sensor keys), the history route and its client function, the sparkline column on `/host`, and the guards for retention, inventory failure and the route's refusals.
